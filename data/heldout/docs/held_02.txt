Vaccination Tracker: meeting notes (12 March)

Attendees: Meera, Dev, Anil

Decisions:
- Parents can record each vaccine given to their child.
- The app sends a reminder when a dose is due soon.
- Health workers can view the vaccination history of any child in their area.
- Only health workers can edit a vaccine record once it is saved.
- Parents can edit a vaccine record at any time.
- Records must be stored on servers located in India.
- The app must be fast even in villages with poor network.
- The government immunisation database will provide the vaccine schedule.
- Assumption: every child has a unique birth registration number.

Open:
- How many children will the system need to support?
