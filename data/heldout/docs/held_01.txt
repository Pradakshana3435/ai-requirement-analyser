Subject: Admission portal requirements

Hi team,

Following yesterday's call with the registrar, here is what the college wants from the new admission portal.

Applicants should be able to fill the application form online and save it as a draft before submitting. The portal must allow upload of mark sheets and a photo. Applicants must pay the application fee before the form can be submitted.

The fee is Rs 500 for all categories. The fee is Rs 300 for reserved categories. The portal needs to be really easy to use for first-time applicants.

Results will be published on the website once the registrar approves them. We are assuming that all applicants have access to a smartphone or computer. The portal will depend on the university's existing payment gateway.

Has anyone confirmed whether we need to support regional languages?

Please let me know if I missed anything.
