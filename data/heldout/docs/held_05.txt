Water Tanker Booking (draft from the ward office)

* Residents book a tanker through the app or by calling the ward office.
* Booking is possible only for residents of the ward.
* Each household can book at most 2 tankers per month.
* Each household can book at most 4 tankers per month.
* The tanker should reach the house in a reasonable time.
* Residents get an SMS when the tanker is on the way.
* Payment is collected on delivery.
* Tanker drivers can mark a delivery as completed in the app.
* The ward office can see all bookings on a map.
* We assume that tankers are available throughout the year.
* The app uses the city GIS service for the map.
* Do we need to handle bookings for apartment complexes differently?
* The system must be secure.
