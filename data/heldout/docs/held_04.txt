Hospital Pharmacy Stock System

1. Pharmacists can issue medicines to a ward and the system must record the batch number.
2. The system must alert the pharmacist when stock of any medicine drops below its reorder level.
3. Expired medicines must never be issued.
4. Stock reports must be available quickly.
5. Narcotic medicines can only be issued with a doctor's digital signature.
6. The system must keep an audit trail of every stock change.
7. The system will be hosted on the hospital's private cloud.
8. Stock counts are synchronised with the central store.
9. Medicines can be returned to the pharmacy.
10. Who approves the reorder of high-value drugs?
11. The system must integrate with the existing billing module.
12. Any medicine can be issued to a ward without extra approval.
