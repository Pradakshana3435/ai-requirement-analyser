E-Learning Portal

REQ-01: Students can enrol in courses and view course materials.
REQ-02: Instructors can upload videos, slides and assignments.
REQ-03: Videos must be encoded so they play on low bandwidth connections.
REQ-04: Students must score at least 40% to pass a course.
REQ-05: Students must score at least 50% to pass a course.
REQ-06: The system must generate certificates for completed courses.
REQ-07: The portal must support 2,000 concurrent students without degradation.
REQ-08: The system relies on the college single sign on service.
