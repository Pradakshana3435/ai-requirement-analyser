Newsletter Platform

- Editors can compose and schedule newsletters.
- Subscribers can unsubscribe with a single click.
- Subscribers must confirm their email address before receiving newsletters.
- Emails should be delivered quickly.
- The platform must send at most 1 newsletter per week to a subscriber.
- The platform must send at least 3 newsletters per week to a subscriber.
- Analytics are available for each newsletter.
- The platform depends on a third party email service.
