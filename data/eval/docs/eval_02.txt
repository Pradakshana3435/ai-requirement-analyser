Hospital Appointment Booking

- Patients can book an appointment with a doctor online.
- Patients can book an appointment with a doctor through the website.
- The system must send a reminder before the appointment.
- Doctors can mark themselves unavailable for a date range.
- The booking page must load quickly.
- A patient cannot hold two appointments at the same time.
- Patient records must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
- Assumption: each doctor works fixed 30 minute slots.
