Insurance Claims Portal

REQ-01: Policyholders can submit a claim with supporting documents.
REQ-02: Policyholders can upload photos and PDFs as supporting documents for a claim.
REQ-03: Claims above 50,000 rupees must be reviewed by a senior adjuster.
REQ-04: Claims are processed efficiently.
REQ-05: Adjusters can approve, reject or request more information on a claim.
REQ-06: The system must keep an audit log of every status change.
REQ-07: Policyholders are notified of decisions.
REQ-08: Assumption: policy data is available in the policy administration system.
