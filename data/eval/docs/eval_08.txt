Hotel Booking System

Guests can search rooms by date, location and price range. Guests can cancel a booking free of charge up to 48 hours before check in. Guests can cancel a booking free of charge up to 24 hours before check in.

The system should provide a great booking experience. Hotel managers can update room prices and availability. Confirmation emails must be sent after a successful booking.

The system must be built on the company's existing cloud platform. Assumption: room rates are quoted in a single currency.
