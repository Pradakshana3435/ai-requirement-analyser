Food Delivery Platform

- Customers can browse restaurants near their location.
- Customers can filter restaurants by cuisine and rating.
- Customers can track their order status in real time.
- Orders should be delivered as soon as possible.
- Restaurants must confirm an order within 5 minutes or it is cancelled automatically.
- Delivery partners are assigned based on proximity.
- The platform depends on the payment gateway for online payments.
- Assumption: restaurants keep their menus up to date.
- Customers can browse restaurants close to where they are.
