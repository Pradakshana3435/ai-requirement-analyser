Gym Membership App

- Members can purchase or renew a membership in the app.
- Members can book a class up to 7 days in advance.
- A class booking can be cancelled up to 2 hours before the class starts.
- Members get a discount if they refer a friend.
- The app should look modern.
- The front desk can check a member in by scanning a QR code.
- The app depends on the turnstile vendor's API for door access.
- Does the gym want to support family plans?
