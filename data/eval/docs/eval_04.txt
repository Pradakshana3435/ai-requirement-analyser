HR Leave Management

Employees can apply for leave through the portal. Managers must approve or reject a leave request within 3 working days. The system should notify the relevant people when needed.

Each employee gets 24 days of paid leave per year. Unused leave may be carried forward to the next year. The portal must be available 99.5% of the time during working hours.

The portal integrates with the existing payroll system. Assumption: all employees have a company email address.
