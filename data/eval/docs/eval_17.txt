Telemedicine Platform

REQ-01: Patients can start a video consultation with an available doctor.
REQ-02: Video quality must adapt to the patient's bandwidth.
REQ-03: Doctors can write and send e-prescriptions.
REQ-04: Consultation recordings are never stored.
REQ-05: Consultation recordings are stored for 5 years.
REQ-06: The platform must be HIPAA compliant.
REQ-07: Patients are charged after the consultation.
REQ-08: The platform should be available at all times.
