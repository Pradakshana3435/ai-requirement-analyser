Ride Hailing App

REQ-01: Riders must be able to request a ride from their current location.
REQ-02: The app shall show the estimated arrival time of the driver.
REQ-03: Drivers must be able to accept or decline a ride request.
REQ-04: Payment is made in cash only.
REQ-05: Riders can pay by card or wallet inside the app.
REQ-06: The app should handle a large number of users.
REQ-07: The app depends on a third party maps service for routing.
REQ-08: Fares are calculated based on distance and time.
REQ-09: What happens when no driver is available nearby?
