Airline Check-in

- Passengers can check in online between 48 hours and 2 hours before departure.
- Passengers can check in online from 24 hours before departure.
- Passengers can select a seat during check in.
- The system shall generate a boarding pass as a PDF and as a QR code.
- The check in process should be fast.
- Extra baggage is charged at the standard rate.
- The system depends on the airline reservation system for passenger data.
- The service must handle peak load around holidays.
