School Attendance System

- Teachers can mark attendance for each class period.
- Parents receive a message when their child is marked absent.
- A student with attendance below 75% is not allowed to sit the final exam.
- Attendance data should be stored for an appropriate period.
- The system must be reliable.
- Teachers can correct an attendance entry within the same day.
- Reports are generated for the principal.
- Assumption: every parent has registered a mobile number.
